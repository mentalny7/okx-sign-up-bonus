The Mystery Box is the signature part of the OKX sign-up bonus. You earn boxes by completing tasks, then open them to reveal a reward. The contents are random within a set range, and the range is shown in the campaign description.

How to use a futures trial bonus without getting burned

Many OKX welcome packages include a trial bonus or position voucher for futures. It lets you open a leveraged position without using your own money for the margin. Sounds free, and in a way it is, but it's easy to waste. A few rules help:

  • Read the voucher terms first. Most have a maximum leverage, a list of allowed contracts and a short expiry, sometimes only a few days.
  • Start with low leverage. A trial voucher at maximum leverage gets liquidated by a normal price move. The voucher is gone and you've learned nothing.
  • Use a stop-loss and a take-profit. Setting both when you open the position is good practice for every trade later.
  • Know what you can keep. Usually the profit from a trial position can be withdrawn and the voucher amount can't. Losses are taken from the voucher, not your balance.
  • Treat it as practice. The real value of a trial bonus is learning how margin, funding and liquidation work without risking your own funds.

Spotting fake OKX bonus offers

Big bonus figures attract scammers, and "OKX bonus" is one of their favourite hooks. Be careful with:

  • Messages on Telegram, WhatsApp or X saying you've "won" an OKX bonus and need to pay a fee or tax to release it.
  • Sites on domains other than okx.com that ask you to log in to claim a reward.
  • "Support agents" who contact you first and ask for your 2FA code or seed phrase.
  • Offers to "double" any deposit you send to a wallet address.

Real OKX rewards show up only in the Rewards Center inside your account. If it isn't there, it isn't real.
