## Rules that decide whether you actually get the bonus

These conditions trip up more new users than anything else. The exact wording is in each campaign's terms, so read them, but this is what to look for:

**KYC is required.** Almost every new-user reward on OKX needs a verified account. An unverified account can see the tasks but can't claim them.

**Tasks have a time window.** A typical window runs from 7 to 30 days after registration. The clock usually starts at sign-up, not at verification. Verifying late costs you days.

**One account per person.** Rewards are for unique users. Creating several accounts with family members' documents, or reusing devices and IPs in suspicious ways, can lead to rewards being cancelled.

**Some rewards aren't withdrawable cash.** Bonuses may come as fee vouchers, futures trial funds or position vouchers. These can be used for trading, and any profit you make can often be withdrawn, but the bonus amount itself usually can't be.

**Deposit tasks count net deposits.** If a task asks you to deposit a set amount, depositing and withdrawing right away may not count, or may cancel the reward. Look for words like "net deposit" or "maintain balance".

**Volume tasks may count specific products only.** "Trade $X of futures" won't be met with spot trades, and the other way round. Some tasks exclude certain pairs, such as stablecoin-to-stablecoin trades.

**Regional availability.** Campaign rewards differ by country, and some regions don't get certain bonuses at all. The referral fee kickback still applies wherever you can trade.
